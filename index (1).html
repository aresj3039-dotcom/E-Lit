Artist's Note

Welcome! Storytelling is dictated by language but what happens when the language we wish to use is dicated to us? Can we be creative with constraints? Can we communicate our thoughts and emotions even in the confines of limited language? This game was designed for anyone who has ever wanted to create a poem but didn't know where to start. Spin up a verse, keep the lines that sing to you, and swap out the rest.

There are no wrong answers here. Play with the words, make something strange or beautiful, and enjoy.

— Jennifer
